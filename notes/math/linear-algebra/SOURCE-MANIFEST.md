# MATH 217 Linear Algebra source manifest

## Scope and evidence

This manifest covers the fixed selected personal body corpus under
`linear-algebra-math217/`: thirteen worksheet PDFs (23 pages) and ten
personal `finished` homework PDFs (205 pages), for **23 body-authoritative
PDFs and 228 visually reviewed source pages**. Every listed source page was
rendered and checked against its visible PDF page. OCR, when used for page
navigation, was not treated as an authority.

The worksheet migration is recorded below. The homework rows are additionally
accounted for by these mutually exclusive visual-migration receipts:

- `migration-batches/homeworks-hw01-hw03.md` -- HW01--HW03, 63 pages.
- `migration-batches/homeworks-hw04-hw06.md` -- HW04--HW06, 72 pages.
- `migration-batches/homeworks-hw07-hw10.md` -- HW07--HW10, 70 pages.

Thus, the homework total is `63 + 72 + 70 = 205` pages. The Typst text keeps
the source language: English remains English, Chinese remains Chinese, and
mixed-language annotations remain mixed-language. Formulae, personal worked
steps, and visible source omissions are transcribed from these selected
personal PDFs, not reconstructed from a definition/theorem handout.

Only the named personal worksheet and `finished` homework PDFs are body
authority. `reference-only` files, and any `raw`, `sol`, or `checking-only`
copies in the review material, are non-body comparison material: they cannot
add a chapter/homework assertion or replace a missing personal response.
