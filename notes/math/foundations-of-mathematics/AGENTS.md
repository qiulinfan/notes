# Foundations of Mathematics

- Author `.tex` sources only and use `../toolchain/latex/qlnotes-native`.
- Enderton's *A Mathematical Introduction to Logic* (second edition, 2001)
  and *Elements of Set Theory* (1977) are the primary reading references.
  Chapter references follow the publishers' contents, not unverified page numbers.
- This course contains original introductory exposition. Distinguish complete
  proofs, proof outlines and reading tasks. Never present an outline as a
  completed proof or claim the whole textbooks have been covered.
- The working conventions are classical first-order logic with equality,
  nonempty domains and ZFC. Specify assignments, free-variable conditions,
  ambient sets and uses of Choice; keep object language and metalanguage distinct.
- Natural deduction here is a pedagogical presentation. Do not identify its
  rule-by-rule syntax with Enderton's axiomatic calculus.
- Theory names, cardinal/ordinal notation and assertion of consistency must
  retain their assumptions. Do not infer ZFC's consistency from completeness.
- The course remains unpublished in `courses.json` until publication is requested.
- No knowledge-base records are generated by course authoring. Source
  registration and record extraction require their own authorized workflows.
- Validate this course with `make -C notes/math/foundations-of-mathematics`
  and `python3 notes/math/toolchain/scripts/check_native_tex.py
  --course foundations-of-mathematics`, then run repository `make check`.
