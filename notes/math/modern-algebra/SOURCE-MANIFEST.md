# Modern Algebra source manifest

## Scope and handling rule

This Typst-first course is the sole canonical destination for the fixed
selected personal Modern Algebra corpus. Its body authority is exactly twelve
personal worksheet PDFs (25 pages) and ten personal homework PDFs (163 pages):
**22 body-authoritative PDFs and 188 visually reviewed body pages**. The
worksheet and homework tables below account for all of those pages.

All handwritten formulae, source language, and visible omissions were checked
from rendered PDF pages. OCR was navigation assistance only, never authority.
Chinese titles, explanations, annotations, prompts, and solutions remain
Chinese; English remains English; mixed-language source material remains
mixed-language.

The ten homework rows are complete according to these mutually exclusive
visual-migration receipts:

- `migration-batches/homeworks-hw01-hw02.md` -- HW01--HW02, 63 pages.
- `migration-batches/homeworks-hw03-hw06.md` -- HW03--HW06, 52 pages.
- `migration-batches/homeworks-hw07-hw10.md` -- HW07--HW10, 48 pages.

Therefore, the homework total is `63 + 52 + 48 = 163` pages. The nine
`reference-only` definition/theorem PDFs (32 pages) were separately read-only
checked against their named targets. They are comparison sources for notation,
ordering, and statement wording only; none is body authority and none supplies
a new body section or an answer missing from a personal PDF.

Status meanings:

- **Complete** -- every listed personal body-source page was visually checked
  and represented in the stated Typst target, including source-visible
  omissions where applicable.
- **Reference checked (non-body)** -- every listed comparison page was
  visually checked only for the stated notation/statement comparison. It was
  not transcribed as body content.
