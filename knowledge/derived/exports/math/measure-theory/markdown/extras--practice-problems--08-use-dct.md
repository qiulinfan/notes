# Use DCT

Let

$$
f \in L_{1}(\lbrack 0,1\rbrack,dx)
$$

Find:

$$
\lim\limits_{n\rightarrow\infty}\frac{1}{n}\int_{0}^{1}\log\left( {1 + e^{nf(x)}} \right)dx
$$
