# Use FTC and Holder

Let $f:\lbrack 0,1\rbrack\rightarrow R$ be absolutely continuous, satisfy $f(0) = 0$ and $f' \in L^{2}(\lbrack 0,1\rbrack)$. Show that

$$
\lim\limits_{x\rightarrow 0 +}x^{- 1/2}f(x)
$$

exists and determine the value of this limit.
