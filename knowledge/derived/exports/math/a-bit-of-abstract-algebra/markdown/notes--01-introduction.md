# Introduction

This is a deliberately personal and incomplete tour of abstract algebra. It groups course worksheets and homework submissions on divisibility, congruence, rings, polynomials, groups, quotient structures, isomorphism theorems, and a short final encounter with elliptic curves.

The original `modern-algebra/` source manifest and migration receipts remain the provenance authority. Visible mistakes, grading feedback, blank answers, and source-authored omissions are retained instead of silently corrected.
