# 任意 σ-algebra 的 intersection 仍是 σ-algebra

## Summary

Let ${ S_alpha }_(alpha in A)$ be a collection of $sigma$-algebra on $X$, then $inter.big_(alpha in A) S_alpha$ is a $sigma$-algebra on $X$.
