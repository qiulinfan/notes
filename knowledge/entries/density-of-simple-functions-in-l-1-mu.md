# density of simple functions in L^1(mu)

## Summary

令 $\(X\,cal(A)\,mu\)$ 为一个 measure space, 令 $f in L^1\(mu\)$, \ 对于任意 $epsilon.alt > 0$, 都存在 simple $phi.alt : X arrow.r bb(C)$ in $L^1\(mu\)$, 使得 $ integral\|f - phi.alt\|< epsilon.alt $
