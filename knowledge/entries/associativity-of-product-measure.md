# associativity of product measure

## Summary

总有 $ cal(A)_1 times.o cal(A)_2 times.o cal(A)_3 =\(cal(A)_1 times.o cal(A)_2\)times.o cal(A)_3 = cal(A)_1 times.o\(cal(A)_2 times.o cal(A)_3\) $ 并且, if $mu_1\,mu_2\,mu_3$ are $sigma$-finite, then: $ mu_1 times mu_2 times mu_3 =\(mu_1 times mu_2\)times mu_3 = mu_1 times\(mu_2 times mu_3\) $
