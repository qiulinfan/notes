# monotone convergence theorem

## Summary

Let ${ f_n }_(n in bb(N))$ be a seq in $L^(+)\(mu\)$, 并且有 $f_n lt.eq f_(n + 1)$ for each $n$. \ 我们 define:$ f := lim_n f_n \(= sup_n f_n\) $, 则一定有 $ integral f = lim_(n arrow.r oo) integral f_n $
