# independence arrow.r.double.long expectation is closed under product

## Summary

对 independent family {X_i} 的任意有限子族与可积 Borel transforms g_j，乘积的 expectation 分解为 E[product_j g_j(X_j)]=product_j E[g_j(X_j)]。
