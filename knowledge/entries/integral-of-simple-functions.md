# integral of simple functions 的性质

## Summary

Let $phi.alt\,psi$ be simple functions in $L^(+)\(mu\)$, 有: - homogeneity: 对于任意非负 $c$, 有 $integral c phi.alt = c integral phi.alt$ - linearity: $integral\(phi.alt + psi\)= integral phi.alt + integral psi$ - monotonicity: $phi.alt lt.eq psi arrow.r.double.long integral phi.alt lt.eq integral psi$ - induced measure: $A mapsto integral_A phi.alt d mu$ 是一个 $cal(M)$ 上的 measure.
