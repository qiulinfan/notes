# LDT meets LRNT: computing RN derivative on bb(R)^n

## Summary

Let $nu$ be a regular Borel measure on $bb(R)^n$, with LRN decomposition $ nu = lambda + rho\,quad d rho = f thin d m\,quad lambda tack.t m $即$ d nu = d lambda + f thin d m $ 那么: 对于 $m$-a.e. $x in bb(R)^n$, 都有: $ lim_(r arrow.r 0) frac(nu\(B\(x\,r\)\), m\(B\(x\,r\)\)) = f\(x\) $
