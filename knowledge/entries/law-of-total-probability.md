# law of total probability

## Summary

若 (Aᵢ) 是 pairwise disjoint events 且其并为 Ω，则任意 event E 满足 P(E)=∑ᵢP(Aᵢ)P(E|Aᵢ)。该公式把 E 按 partition (Aᵢ) 分解并求和。
