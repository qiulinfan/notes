# decomposing probability of intersection of events

## Summary

对 events A₁,…,Aₙ，交集概率可逐步分解为 P(A₁)P(A₂|A₁)⋯P(Aₙ|A₁∩⋯∩Aₙ₋₁)。这是 conditional probability 定义的连锁展开。
