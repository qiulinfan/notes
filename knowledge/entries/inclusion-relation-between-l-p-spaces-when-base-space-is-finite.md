# inclusion relation between L^p spaces (when base space is finite measure)

## Summary

如果 measure space $X$ has finite measure, 那么有 $ L^oo\(X\)subset dots.h.c subset L^m\(X\)subset dots.h.c subset L^n\(X\)subset dots.h.c $ for 任意的 $m gt.eq n$.
