# dominated convergence theorem

## Summary

Let $\(f_n\)$ be a seq of functions in $L^1\(mu\)$, s.t. - $f_n arrow.r f$ a.e. - 存在 $g in L^1\(mu\)$ s.t. $\|f_n\|lt.eq g$ a.e. for all $n$. Claim: $f in L^1\(mu\)$ 并且 $ integral f = lim_n integral f_n $
