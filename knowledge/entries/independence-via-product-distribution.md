# independence via product distribution of marginal distributions

## Summary

random variables X,Y independent 是指任意 Borel sets A,B 都满足 P(X in A,Y in B)=P(X in A)P(Y in B)；等价地，joint cdf 分解为 F_X(x)F_Y(y)。
