# Personal knowledge decisions

These are decisions of this personal instance, not kgdistiller product defaults.

- Identity originates in explicit authored authority markers and reviewed
  mappings. Text or graph similarity retrieves evidence; it does not establish
  identity.
- Ambiguous mappings remain `uncertain`, contradictory mappings remain
  `conflict`, and neither state is auto-promoted to a global alias.
- Paper distillation uses an isolated namespace by default and must not modify
  the personal graph, alignments, notes, or public site without a separate,
  reviewed ingest request.
- Do not import solvablemodel paper nodes C02, C07, C09, C12, C15, C16, C17,
  C19, C20, or other paper nodes into this personal graph/research
  authority under the prior evaluation decision.
- Do not write knowledge markers back into the solvablemodel paper sources
  under the prior evaluation decision.
- The current private graph contains durable agent-authored entries, evidence,
  and semantic edges for which no separate decision log exists. Preserve its
  bytes across this repository separation; note-only reconstruction is not an
  acceptable migration.
