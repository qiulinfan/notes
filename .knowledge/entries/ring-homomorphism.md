A map from one ring to another is a ring homomorphism when it preserves addition and multiplication.

## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{Ring homomorphism}}
>
> A map \(\varphi:R\rightarrow S\) is a ring homomorphism when \(\varphi\left( {x + y} \right) = \varphi(x) + \varphi(y)\) and \(\varphi\left( {xy} \right) = \varphi(x)\varphi(y)\).
>
> \end{definition}
