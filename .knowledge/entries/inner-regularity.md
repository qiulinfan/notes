## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{\kn{\textbf{inner regularity}}}
>
> 对于一个 Lebesgue-Stieltjes measure associated with \(F\), 任意 \(E \in \mathcal{M}_{\mu}\), 它的 measure 等于:
>
> \[\mu_{F}(E) = \sup\left\{ {\mu_{F}(K) \mid K\ \text{compact , and}\ K \subseteq E} \right\}\]
>
> \end{theorem}
