## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{\kn{Lusin's Theorem}}
>
> If \(f:\lbrack a,b\rbrack\rightarrow\mathbb{C}\) 是 Leb. mble 的, 那么 \(\forall\varepsilon > 0\), 都存在 compact \(K \subseteq \lbrack a,b\rbrack\) s.t. \(m(K^{c}) < \varepsilon\) 并且 \(f|_{K}\) ctn.
>
> \end{theorem}
