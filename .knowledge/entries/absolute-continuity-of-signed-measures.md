给定 p.m. $mu$ 和 [signed measure] $nu$ on $\(X\,cal(A)\)$, 我们称 $nu$ is absolutely continuous w.r.t. $mu$, 如果 $ forall E in cal(A)\,quad mu\(E\)= 0 arrow.r.double.long nu\(E\)= 0 $ 即: $nu$ 的 null sets 包含了 $mu$ 的所有 null sets. ($nu$ 拥有比 $mu$ 严格更多的 null sets) \ 写作 $ nu lt.double mu $

## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{absolute continuity of signed measures}}
>
> 给定 p.m. \(\mu\) 和 \knref{signed measure} \(\nu\) on \((X,\mathcal{A})\), 我们称 \(\nu\) is absolutely continuous w.r.t. \(\mu\), 如果
>
> \[\forall E \in \mathcal{A},\quad\mu(E) = 0\Longrightarrow\nu(E) = 0\]
>
> 即: \(\nu\) 的 null sets 包含了 \(\mu\) 的所有 null sets. (\(\nu\) 拥有比 \(\mu\) 严格更多的 null sets)\\
> 写作
>
> \[\nu \ll \mu\]
>
> \end{definition}
