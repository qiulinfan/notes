> \begin{corollary}{\kn{Minkowski inequality}}
>
> 对于任意 \(1 \leq p < \infty\), 都有:
>
> \[\parallel f + g \parallel \leq \parallel f\underset{p}{\parallel} + \parallel g\underset{p}{\parallel}\]
>
> \end{corollary}
