# Inner product space

## Summary

A vector space equipped with an inner product, illustrated by function-space examples.

## Evidence

```
For the \kn{Inner product space} \(V\) of functions on \(\left\lbrack {- \pi,\pi} \right\rbrack\), \(< f,g \geq \frac{1}{\pi}\int_{- \pi}^{\pi}f(t)g(t)\, dt\):
```
