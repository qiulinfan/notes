Let $phi.alt\,psi$ be simple functions in $L^(+)\(mu\)$, 有: - homogeneity: 对于任意非负 $c$, 有 $integral c phi.alt = c integral phi.alt$ - linearity: $integral\(phi.alt + psi\)= integral phi.alt + integral psi$ - monotonicity: $phi.alt lt.eq psi arrow.r.double.long integral phi.alt lt.eq integral psi$ - induced measure: $A mapsto integral_A phi.alt d mu$ 是一个 $cal(M)$ 上的 measure.

## Evidence

> \begin{proposition}{\kn{integral of simple functions 的性质}}
>
> Let \(\varphi,\psi\) be simple functions in \(L^{+}(\mu)\), 有:
>
> \begin{itemize}
> \item
>   \textbf{homogeneity:} 对于任意非负 \(c\), 有 \(\int c\varphi = c\int\varphi\)
> \item
>   \textbf{linearity:} \(\int(\varphi + \psi) = \int\varphi + \int\psi\)
> \item
>   \textbf{monotonicity:} \(\varphi \leq \psi\Longrightarrow\int\varphi \leq \int\psi\)
> \item
>   \textbf{induced measure}: \(A\mapsto\int_{A}\varphi d\mu\) 是一个 \(\mathcal{M}\) 上的 measure.
> \end{itemize}
>
> \end{proposition}
