## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{\kn{HL maximal theorem}}
>
> For \(L^{1}(m^{n})\), take constant \(C := 3^{n}\), 则对于任意 \(f \in L^{1}(m^{n})\), 都有:
>
> \[\left. m(\left\{ {x:Hf(x) > \alpha} \right\}) \leq \frac{C}{\alpha}\int \middle| f| \right.\]
>
> \end{theorem}
