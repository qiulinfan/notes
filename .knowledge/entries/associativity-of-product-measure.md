# associativity of product measure

## Summary

总有 $ cal(A)_1 times.o cal(A)_2 times.o cal(A)_3 =\(cal(A)_1 times.o cal(A)_2\)times.o cal(A)_3 = cal(A)_1 times.o\(cal(A)_2 times.o cal(A)_3\) $ 并且, if $mu_1\,mu_2\,mu_3$ are $sigma$-finite, then: $ mu_1 times mu_2 times mu_3 =\(mu_1 times mu_2\)times mu_3 = mu_1 times\(mu_2 times mu_3\) $

## Evidence

```
\begin{corollary}{\kn{associativity of product measure}}

总有

\[\mathcal{A}_{1} \otimes \mathcal{A}_{2} \otimes \mathcal{A}_{3} = (\mathcal{A}_{1} \otimes \mathcal{A}_{2}) \otimes \mathcal{A}_{3} = \mathcal{A}_{1} \otimes (\mathcal{A}_{2} \otimes \mathcal{A}_{3})\]

并且, if \(\mu_{1},\mu_{2},\mu_{3}\) are \(\sigma\)-finite, then:

\[\mu_{1} \times \mu_{2} \times \mu_{3} = (\mu_{1} \times \mu_{2}) \times \mu_{3} = \mu_{1} \times (\mu_{2} \times \mu_{3})\]

\end{corollary}
```
