# dual space is always Banach

## Summary

对于任意的 NVS $V$: $V^(*)$ 都是一个 Banach space. (not assuming $V$ Banach).

## Evidence

```
\begin{theorem}{\kn{dual space is always Banach}}

对于\textbf{任意的 NVS} \(V\): \(V^{\ast}\) 都是一个 Banach space. (not assuming \(V\) Banach).

\end{theorem}
```
