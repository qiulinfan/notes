The notes state that the real numbers are the unique complete ordered field.

## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{The unique \kn{Complete ordered field}}
>
> \(\mathbb{R}\) is the unique complete ordered field. Also, \(\mathbb{N}\) is the intersection of all inductive subsets of \(\mathbb{R}\).
>
> \end{theorem}
