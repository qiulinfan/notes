一个 regular Borel measure $nu$ on $bb(R)^n$ 一定满足: + outer regularity: $ nu\(E\)= inf { nu\(U\)divides E subset U upright(" open") } $ + inner regularity: $ nu\(E\)= inf { nu\(U\)divides E subset U upright(" open") } $

## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{\kn{regular Borel measure: 蕴含了 regularity}}
>
> 一个 regular Borel measure \(\nu\) on \(\mathbb{R}^{n}\) 一定满足:
>
> \begin{enumerate}
> \item
>   outer regularity:
>
>   \[\nu(E) = \inf\left\{ {\nu(U) \mid E \subset U\ \text{open}} \right\}\]
> \item
>   inner regularity:
>
>   \[\nu(E) = \inf\left\{ {\nu(U) \mid E \subset U\ \text{open}} \right\}\]
> \end{enumerate}
>
> \end{theorem}
