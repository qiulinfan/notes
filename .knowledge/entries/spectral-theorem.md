> \begin{theorem}{\kn{Spectral theorem}}
>
> \(A\) is orthogonally diagonalizable iff \(A\) is symmetric: \(\exists S \in \mathbb{R}^{n \times n}\) such that \(S^{- 1} = S^{t}\) and \(S^{t}AS\) is diagonal iff \(A\) is symmetric.
>
> \end{theorem}
