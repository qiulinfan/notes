A projector whose kernel is orthogonal to its image; the notes also characterize it by P = P*.

## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{Orthogonal projector}}
>
> 我们称一个 projector 是 orthogonal projector，如果 \(\text{ker}(P) \perp \text{im}(P)\).
>
> \end{definition}
