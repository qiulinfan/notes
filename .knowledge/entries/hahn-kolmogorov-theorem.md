给定一个 premeasure $mu_0$ on algebra $cal(M)_0$ of $X$, 以及其 induced outer measure $mu *$, 我们令 按 [$sigma$-algebra generated by a subset] 的定义, $ cal(M) := < cal(M)_0 > $ 表示 $sigma$-algebra generated by the algebra $cal(M)_0$. \ 并令 $ mu := mu^(*)\|_(cal(M)) $ then we have: + $\(X\,cal(M)_0\,mu_0\)$ extends to $\(X\,cal(M)\,mu\)$ \ 即: $mu\|_(cal(M)_0)= mu_0$ + $mu\|_(cal(M))$ 是 the largest extension of $mu_0$ to $cal(M)$ (即: 对于任意其他的 $cal(M)$ 上的 measure $nu$ that extends $mu_0$ to $cal(M)$, 都有 $nu\(E\)lt.eq mu\(E\)$ for all $E in cal(M)$); \ 并且 if $mu_0$ is $sigma$-finite, 则 $mu$ 是 the unique extension of $mu_0$ to $cal(M)$.

## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{\kn{Hahn--Kolmogorov theorem}}
>
> 给定一个 premeasure \(\mu_{0}\) on algebra \(\mathcal{M}_{0}\) of \(X\), 以及其 induced outer measure \(\mu \ast\), 我们令 按 \knref{$\sigma$-algebra generated by a subset} 的定义,
>
> \[\mathcal{M} := < \mathcal{M}_{0} >\]
>
> 表示 \(\sigma\)-algebra generated by the algebra \(\mathcal{M}_{0}\).\\
> 并令
>
> \[\mu := \mu^{\ast}|_{\mathcal{M}}\]
>
> then we have:
>
> \begin{enumerate}
> \item
>   \((X,\mathcal{M}_{0},\mu_{0})\) extends to \((X,\mathcal{M},\mu)\)\\
>   即: \(\left. \mu \middle| {}_{\mathcal{M}_{0}} = \mu_{0} \right.\)
> \item
>   \(\mu|_{\mathcal{M}}\) 是 \textbf{the largest extension of \(\mu_{0}\) to \(\mathcal{M}\)} (即: 对于任意其他的 \(\mathcal{M}\) 上的 measure \(\nu\) that extends \(\mu_{0}\) to \(\mathcal{M}\), 都有 \(\nu(E) \leq \mu(E)\) for all \(E \in \mathcal{M}\));\\
>   并且 \textbf{if \(\mu_{0}\) is \(\sigma\)-finite}, 则 \(\mu\) 是 \textbf{the unique extension} of \(\mu_{0}\) to \(\mathcal{M}\).
> \end{enumerate}
>
> \end{theorem}
