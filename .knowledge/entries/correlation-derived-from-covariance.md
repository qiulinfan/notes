739–832：correlation 定义为标准化 covariance。

## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{correlation}}
>
> \[\mathit{\rho}(\mathit{X},\mathit{Y}) = \frac{\text{Cov}(X,Y)}{\sqrt{\text{Var}(\mathit{X})}\sqrt{\text{Var}(\mathit{Y})}}\]
>
> \end{definition}
