## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{density of a set at a point}}
>
> 对于 \(E \subset \mathbb{R}^{n}\) Lebesgue measurable (which implies: \(\chi_{E} \in L_{loc}^{1}\)), 我们定义:
>
> \[D_{E}(x): = \lim\limits_{r\rightarrow 0^{+}}\frac{m(E \cap B(x,r))}{m(B(x,r))}\]
>
> \end{definition}
