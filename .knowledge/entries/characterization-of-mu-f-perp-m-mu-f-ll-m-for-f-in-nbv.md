# characterization of μ_F perp m 和 μ_F ll m, for F in NBV

## Summary

对于 $F in N B V$, 我们已经知道: $F'$ $m$-a.e. 存在, 且 $F' in L^1\(m\)$. \ Now we claim, 有: $ mu_F perp m arrow.l.r.double F' = 0 quad m upright("-a.e.") $以及 $ mu_F lt.double m arrow.l.r.double F\(x\)= integral_(- oo)^x F'\(t\)thin d t quad forall x $

## Evidence

```
\begin{theorem}{\kn{characterization of $\mu_F \perp m$ 和 $\mu_F \ll m$, for
$F \in NBV$}}

对于 \(F \in NBV\), 我们已经知道: \(F'\) \(m\)-a.e. 存在, 且 \(F' \in L^{1}(m)\).\\
Now we claim, 有:

\[\mu_{F} \perp m\Leftrightarrow F' = 0\quad m\ \text{-a.e.}\]

以及

\[\mu_{F} \ll m\Leftrightarrow F(x) = \int_{- \infty}^{x}F'(t)\, dt\quad\forall x\]

\end{theorem}
```
