# complex measure

## Summary

一个 complex measure on a measurable space $\(X\,cal(A)\)$ 是一个 map $nu : cal(A) arrow.r bb(C)$ satisfying $nu\(diameter\)= 0$ 以及 ctbl disjoint additivity.

## Evidence
