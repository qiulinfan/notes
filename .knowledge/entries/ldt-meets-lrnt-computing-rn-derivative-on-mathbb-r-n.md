Let $nu$ be a regular Borel measure on $bb(R)^n$, with LRN decomposition $ nu = lambda + rho\,quad d rho = f thin d m\,quad lambda tack.t m $即$ d nu = d lambda + f thin d m $ 那么: 对于 $m$-a.e. $x in bb(R)^n$, 都有: $ lim_(r arrow.r 0) frac(nu\(B\(x\,r\)\), m\(B\(x\,r\)\)) = f\(x\) $

## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{\kn{LDT meets LRNT: computing RN derivative on $\mathbb{R}^n$}}
>
> Let \(\nu\) be a regular Borel measure on \(\mathbb{R}^{n}\), with LRN decomposition
>
> \[\nu = \lambda + \rho,\quad d\rho = f\, dm,\quad\lambda\bot m\]
>
> 即
>
> \[d\nu = d\lambda + f\, dm\]
>
> 那么: 对于 \(m\)-a.e. \(x \in \mathbb{R}^{n}\), 都有:
>
> \[\lim\limits_{r\rightarrow 0}\frac{\nu(B(x,r))}{m(B(x,r))} = f(x)\]
>
> \end{theorem}
