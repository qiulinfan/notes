The proof checks that intersection preserves each defining sigma-algebra closure condition.

## Evidence

> \begin{lemma}{\kn{任意 $\sigma$-algebra 的 intersection 仍是 $\sigma$-algebra}}
>
> Let \(\left\{ S_{\alpha} \right\}_{\alpha \in A}\) be a collection of \(\sigma\)-algebra on \(X\), then \(\bigcap_{\alpha \in A}S_{\alpha}\) is a \(\sigma\)-algebra on \(X\).
>
> \end{lemma}
