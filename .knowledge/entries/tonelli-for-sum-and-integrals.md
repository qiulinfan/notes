## Evidence

> \begin{corollary}{\kn{Tonelli for sum and integrals}}
>
> for \(\left\{ f_{i} \right\}_{i \in \mathbb{N}}\) in \(L^{+}(\mu)\), 有:
>
> \[\int\sum\limits_{i = 1}^{\infty}f_{i} = \sum\limits_{i = 1}^{\infty}\int f_{i}\]
>
> \end{corollary}
