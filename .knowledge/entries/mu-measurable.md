Given outer measure $mu^(*)$, 我们称 $A subset.eq X$ 是 $mu^(*)$-measurable 的, if: $ mu^(*)\(E\)= mu^(*)\(E inter A\)+ mu^(*)\(E inter A^c\) $

## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{$\mu^{\ast}$-measurable}}
>
> Given outer measure \(\mu^{\ast}\), 我们称 \(A \subseteq X\) 是 \(\mu^{\ast}\)-measurable 的, if:
>
> \[\mu^{\ast}(E) = \mu^{\ast}(E \cap A) + \mu^{\ast}(E \cap A^{c})\]
>
> \end{definition}
