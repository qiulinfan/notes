## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{real-valued integrable function}}
>
> Given measure space \((X,\mathcal{M},\mu)\), \textbf{measurable \(f:X\rightarrow\bar{\mathbb{R}}\) 被称为 integrable} 的, 如果它满足
>
> \[\left. \int \middle| f \middle| < \infty \right.\]
>
> 并定义其 integral 为:
>
> \[\int f = \int f^{+} - \int f^{-}\]
>
> \end{definition}
