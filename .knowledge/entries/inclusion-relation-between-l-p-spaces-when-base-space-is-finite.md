# inclusion relation between L^p spaces (when base space is finite measure)

## Summary

如果 measure space $X$ has finite measure, 那么有 $ L^oo\(X\)subset dots.h.c subset L^m\(X\)subset dots.h.c subset L^n\(X\)subset dots.h.c $ for 任意的 $m gt.eq n$.

## Evidence

```
\begin{theorem}{\kn{inclusion relation between $L^p$ spaces (when base space is finite
measure)}}

如果 measure space \(X\) has finite measure, 那么有

\[L^{\infty}(X) \subset \cdots \subset L^{m}(X) \subset \cdots \subset L^{n}(X) \subset \cdots\]

for 任意的 \(m \geq n\).

\end{theorem}
```
