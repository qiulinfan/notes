The inclusion claims follow from the generated sigma-algebra being the smallest sigma-algebra containing its generators.

## Evidence

> \begin{lemma}{\kn{inclusion properties of generated $\sigma$-algebra}}\label{lem-01-sigma-algebra-measure-inclusion-properties-of-generated-sigma-algebra}
>
> \begin{enumerate}
> \item
>   if \(\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathcal{A}\) where \(\mathcal{A}\) is a \(\sigma\)-algebra, then \(< \mathcal{E} > \subseteq \mathcal{A}\).
> \item
>   if \(\mathcal{E} \subseteq \mathcal{F}\), then \(< \mathcal{E} > \subseteq < \mathcal{F} >\).
> \item
>   if \(\mathcal{E} \subseteq < \mathcal{F} >\), then \(< \mathcal{E} > \subseteq < \mathcal{F} >\).
> \end{enumerate}
>
> \end{lemma}
