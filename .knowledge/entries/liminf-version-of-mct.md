## Evidence

> \begin{corollary}{\kn{liminf version of MCT}}
>
> suppose \((f_{n})_{n \in \mathbb{N}}\) 是一个 seq of functions in \(L^{+}(\mu)\), 且 \(f_{n}\rightarrow f \in L^{+}(\mu)\), 则:
>
> \[\operatorname{lim\, inf}\limits_{n}\int f_{n} \geq \int f\]
>
> \end{corollary}
