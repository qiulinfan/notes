# density of simple functions in L^1(μ)

## Summary

令 $\(X\,cal(A)\,mu\)$ 为一个 measure space, 令 $f in L^1\(mu\)$, \ 对于任意 $epsilon.alt > 0$, 都存在 simple $phi.alt : X arrow.r bb(C)$ in $L^1\(mu\)$, 使得 $ integral\|f - phi.alt\|< epsilon.alt $

## Evidence

```
\begin{theorem}{\kn{density of simple functions in $L^1(\mu)$}}

令 \((X,\mathcal{A},\mu)\) 为一个 measure space, 令 \(f \in L^{1}(\mu)\),\\
对于任意 \(\varepsilon > 0\), 都存在 simple \(\varphi:X\rightarrow\mathbb{C}\) in \(L^{1}(\mu)\), 使得

\[\left. \int \middle| f - \varphi \middle| < \varepsilon \right.\]

\end{theorem}
```
