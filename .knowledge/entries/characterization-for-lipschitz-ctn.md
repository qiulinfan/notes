# characterization for Lipschitz ctn

## Summary

对于 $F : bb(R) arrow.r bb(C)$, we have: $ F upright(" Lipschitz ctn with const ") M arrow.l.r.double F in A C upright(" and ")\|F'\(x\)\|lt.eq M upright(" a.e.") $

\begin{theorem}{\kn{characterization for Lipschitz ctn}}

对于 \(F:\mathbb{R}\rightarrow\mathbb{C}\), we have:

\[\left. F\ \text{Lipschitz ctn with const}\ M\Leftrightarrow F \in AC\ \text{and} \middle| F'(x) \middle| \leq M\ \text{a.e.} \right.\]
