The corollary constructs the smallest containing sigma-algebra as the intersection of every sigma-algebra containing the collection.

## Evidence

> \begin{corollary}{\kn{unique smallest $\sigma$-algebra containing a collection of subsets}}
>
> Given \(\varepsilon \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X)\)
>
> \[< \varepsilon > := \bigcap\limits_{\varepsilon \subseteq S \subseteq \mathcal{P}(X),\, S\ \text{is}\ \sigma\ \text{-algebra on}\ X}S\]
>
> \end{corollary}
