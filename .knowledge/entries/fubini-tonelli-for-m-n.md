Suppose $f in L^(+)\(bb(R)^n\)$ or $L^1\(bb(R)^n\)$ $ integral f d m^n & = integral dots.h.c integral f\(x_1\,dots.h.c\,x_n\) d x_1 dots.h.c d x_n\ & = integral dots.h.c integral f\(x_1\,dots.h.c\,x_n\) d x_n dots.h.c d x_1 $

## Evidence

> \begin{theorem}{\kn{Fubini-Tonelli for $m^n$}}
>
> Suppose \(f \in L^{+}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\) or \(L^{1}(\mathbb{R}^{n})\)
>
> \[\begin{matrix}
> {\int f dm^{n}} & {= \int\cdots\int f(x_{1},\cdots,x_{n}) dx_{1}\cdots dx_{n}} \\
>  & {= \int\cdots\int f(x_{1},\cdots,x_{n}) dx_{n}\cdots dx_{1}}
> \end{matrix}\]
>
> \end{theorem}
