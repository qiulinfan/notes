## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{essential supremum}}
>
> \[\parallel f\underset{\infty}{\parallel}: = \inf\left\{ {a \geq 0:\mu\left\{ x: \middle| f(x) \middle| > a \right\} = 0} \right\}\]
>
> 也可以写作:
>
> \[\left. \text{ess}\sup\limits_{x \in X} \middle| f(x)| \right.\]
>
> \end{definition}
