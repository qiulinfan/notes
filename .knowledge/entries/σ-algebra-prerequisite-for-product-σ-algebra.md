the product sigma-algebra is generated from factor sigma-algebras

## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{product $\sigma$-algebra}}
>
> Suppose \((X_{i},\mathcal{A}_{i})\) mble, \(1 \leq i \leq n\), the product \(\sigma\)-algebra \(A_{1} \otimes \cdots \otimes A_{n}\) on \(X_{1} \times \cdots \times X_{n}\) is the smallest \knref{sigma-algebra} s.t. the \textbf{coordinate map}
>
> \[\pi_{j}:X_{1} \times \cdots \times X_{n}\rightarrow X_{j}\]
>
> \textbf{is measurable.}\\
> 即 the \(\sigma\)-algebra generated by: \(\left\{ {\pi_{\alpha}(E_{\alpha}):E_{\alpha} \in \mathcal{A}_{\alpha}} \right\}\).
>
> \[A_{1} \otimes \cdots \otimes A_{n}: = < \left\{ {\pi_{\alpha}(E_{\alpha}):E_{\alpha} \in \mathcal{A}_{\alpha}} \right\} >\]
>
> \end{definition}
