# another criterion for Banach space

## Summary

A normed VS $\(V\,\|\|dot.op\|\|\)$ is a Banach space iff every absolutely convergent series converges.

## Evidence

```
\begin{theorem}{\kn{another criterion for Banach space}}\label{thm-09-l-p-space-and-inequalities-another-criterion-for-banach-space}

\end{theorem}
```
