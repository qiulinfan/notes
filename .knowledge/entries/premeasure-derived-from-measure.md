The source describes a premeasure as the countably additive measure axiom restricted to an algebra.

## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{premeasure}}
>
> 给定 \(\mathcal{P}(X)\) 上的一个 \knref{algebra of sets} \(\mathcal{A}_{0}\), 我们称 \(\mu_{0}:\mathcal{A}_{0}\rightarrow\lbrack 0, + \infty\rbrack\) 为一个 premeasure, if
>
> \begin{enumerate}
> \item
>   \(\mu_{0}(\varnothing) = 0\)
> \item
>   \(\mu_{0}\) ctbl disjoint additive in \(\mathcal{A}_{0}\)
> \end{enumerate}
>
> \end{definition}
