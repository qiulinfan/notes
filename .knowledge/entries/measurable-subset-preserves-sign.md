# measurable subset preserves sign

## Summary

measurable subset of a measurable set $F subset E$ preserves the sign of $E$. \ 即: $E$ 是一个 positive / null / negative $arrow.r.double.long$任意 $F subset E$ 是一个 positive / null / negative.

## Evidence

```
\begin{lemma}{\kn{measurable subset preserves sign}}

measurable subset of a measurable set \(F \subset E\) preserves the sign of \(E\).\\
即: \(E\) 是一个 positive / null / negative \(\Longrightarrow\)任意 \(F \subset E\) 是一个 positive / null / negative.

\end{lemma}
```
