## Evidence

> \begin{definition}{\kn{regular Borel measure}}
>
> 一个 Borel measure \(\nu\) on \(\mathbb{R}^{n}\) 被称为 regular 的, if \(\nu\) is locally finite (finite on every compact set).
>
> \end{definition}
